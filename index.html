The evidence has boundaries.

The available measures show theatrical patterns within a frozen corpus. Release context, missingness, and narrative evidence limit broader claims.

Methodology & Sources

Corpus. The frozen catalogue contains 120 theatrical films released from 1998 through 18 August 2026: 29 Walt Disney Animation Studios films, 28 Pixar Animation Studios films, 49 DreamWorks Animation films, and a separate 14-film Walt Disney Pictures remake/reimagining layer.

Analytical sides. “Disney animated (WDAS + Pixar)” aggregates WDAS and Pixar only where the comparison requires a Disney animated side. Pixar remains labeled Pixar Animation Studios. Disney remakes never enter the main animated temporal comparison.

Financial measure. The primary charts use U.S. domestic theatrical gross expressed in July-2026 USD equivalent. Worldwide gross, where referenced in the underlying data, remains nominal; reported production budgets remain estimates. Sparse international gross is not used systematically.

Missingness and release context. TMDB ratings and vote counts are unavailable for all 120 films. Missing adjusted-domestic values are omitted, not replaced with zero. COVID-era and day-and-date releases are retained with context rather than counterfactually corrected. Soul’s U.S. value reflects a later theatrical re-release, and 2026 is partial through 18 August.

Rivalry context. The three early cases are sourced narrative annotations chosen after corpus definition. They are not a quantitative rivalry variable, and Shrek has no assigned Disney counterpart.
