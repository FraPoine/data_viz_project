What Are We Comparing?

Before the rivalry

DreamWorks SKG was founded in 1994 by Steven Spielberg, Jeffrey Katzenberg, and David Geffen. Katzenberg had just left a decade running Walt Disney Studios, giving the new competitor an unusually direct connection to Disney’s animation business.

The Balance Moves Over Time

The central analytical section compares release activity with the five-year adjusted-domestic temporal summary.

Each line point is the median adjusted U.S. domestic gross of films released in the five years ending in that year.

A second Disney strategy enters the story

Alongside its animated releases, Disney increasingly revisited its animated library in live-action or photorealistic form. That strategy becomes visible from 2010 onward—and remains a separate layer rather than part of the Disney animated comparison.

No Single Winner, No Single Measure

The available measures show a changing competitive balance, not a permanent winner.

The balance moves.

Release activity and adjusted-domestic trends shift across the period; neither studio side holds one fixed position.

Measures are complementary.

Different financial measures describe different aspects of theatrical performance, while release counts describe activity rather than revenue.

The evidence has boundaries.

The available measures show theatrical patterns within a frozen corpus. Release context, missingness, and narrative evidence limit broader claims.

Methodology & Sources

Corpus. The frozen catalogue contains 120 theatrical films released from 1998 through 18 August 2026: 29 Walt Disney Animation Studios films, 28 Pixar Animation Studios films, 49 DreamWorks Animation films, and a separate 14-film Walt Disney Pictures remake/reimagining layer.

Analytical sides. “Disney animated (WDAS + Pixar)” aggregates WDAS and Pixar only where the comparison requires a Disney animated side. Pixar remains labeled Pixar Animation Studios. Disney remakes never enter the main animated temporal comparison.

Financial measure. The primary charts use U.S. domestic theatrical gross expressed in July-2026 USD equivalent. Worldwide gross, where referenced in the underlying data, remains nominal; reported production budgets remain estimates. Sparse international gross is not used systematically.

Missingness and release context. TMDB ratings and vote counts are unavailable for all 120 films. Missing adjusted-domestic values are omitted, not replaced with zero. COVID-era and day-and-date releases are retained with context rather than counterfactually corrected. Soul’s U.S. value reflects a later theatrical re-release, and 2026 is partial through 18 August.

Rivalry context. The three early cases are sourced narrative annotations chosen after corpus definition. They are not a quantitative rivalry variable, and Shrek has no assigned Disney counterpart.

Historical context. DreamWorks’ formation and founders are documented in its 2010 SEC filing, while Katzenberg’s Disney tenure is recorded in a later DreamWorks Animation filing. The pre-acquisition Disney–Pixar relationship and 2006 transaction are documented by The Walt Disney Company. Rivalry-item evidence links remain attached to their interactive details.
